## Key Findings

- **Lagos and Abuja** represent the strongest premium housing markets in the dataset.
- **Villas, mansions, penthouses, and duplexes** attract higher average prices.
- Bedrooms and bathrooms have positive but relatively weak relationships with price.
- Furnishing does not consistently guarantee a higher asking price.
- Enterprise listings are not necessarily the most expensive.

## Business Recommendations

- Use **location-specific pricing** when setting asking prices.
- Highlight valuable features such as size, location, security, parking, and finishing.
- Use professional images and detailed descriptions to improve listing visibility.
- Apply targeted marketing strategies in lower-performing regions.
- Use paid promotion strategically rather than assuming that greater visibility automatically means a higher price.
